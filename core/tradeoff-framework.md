# Trade-off Framework

Pros and Cons must be structured, mapped, and resolved.

## Process
1. For every option, identify key positive drivers (Pros) and negative drivers (Cons).
2. Explicitly map Cons to risk mitigations or penalties (e.g. operational effort).
3. The reasoning engine must resolve the trade-offs:
   - Example: "Option A offers fast learning and lower cost, but at the expense of enterprise strategic alignment."
4. Contrast the winner directly with the runner-up in the final report, justifying why the trade-offs favored the winner.
