# Quality Gates

Before finalizing a decision recommendation, the framework must pass all 9 Quality Gates:

1. **Gate 1: Decision Clarity**: Can the question be answered with a choice?
2. **Gate 2: Options Mutually Understandable**: Are the options distinct and comparable?
3. **Gate 3: Evidence Quality**: Is there enough evidence to compare options?
4. **Gate 4: Constraints Defined**: Are the hard constraints explicitly defined?
5. **Gate 5: Criteria Defined**: Are relevant decision profiles and dimensions selected?
6. **Gate 6: Explicit Assumptions**: Are assumptions listed with their potential impact?
7. **Gate 7: Trade-offs Evaluated**: Have pros/cons and dominance been processed?
8. **Gate 8: Robustness Check**: Has sensitivity analysis been computed?
9. **Gate 9: Actionability**: Does the final recommendation map to a concrete next action?
