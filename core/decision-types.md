# Decision Classification Taxonomy

Every incoming decision must be categorized into one of these types:

1. **Reversible (Type 2)**:
   - High reversibility.
   - Low cost to undo.
   - Prefer: Speed to learning, lower confidence threshold.

2. **Irreversible (Type 1)**:
   - Low reversibility, high cost to undo.
   - Requires: Stronger evidence, high confidence.

3. **Exploratory**:
   - Primary goal is info gathering / learning.
   - High learning speed, low cost.

4. **Commitment**:
   - Allocating significant resources to scale an validated channel.

5. **Crisis**:
   - Immediate time urgency. Speed is the absolute highest priority.

6. **Strategic / Operational / Optimization**:
   - Traditional strategy alignment vs daily execution choices.
