# Decision Framework Workflow

Canonical steps to execute on any decision question:

1. **Intake & Question Framing**: Transform ambiguous questions into clear binary or multi-choice statements.
2. **Context Collection**: Gather constraints, ICP, competitive intelligence, strategies, and history.
3. **Option Validation**: Verify if options are structured and have unique IDs.
4. **Evidence Collection**: Distinguish facts, observations, inferences, assumptions, and hypotheses.
5. **Constraint Check (Stage 1 Gating)**: Mark options violating budget/time/effort as INELIGIBLE.
6. **Dominance Analysis**: Eliminate options that perform worse than another option in every criterion.
7. **Criteria Scoring (Stage 2)**: Load selected profile from `decision-profiles.yaml` and calculate scores.
8. **Trade-off Analysis**: Map Pros and Cons logically.
9. **Uncertainty & Sensitivity Analysis**: Identify dependencies on variables, calculate winning margin.
10. **Recommendation Selection**: Pick one clear choice (or recommend deferring to resolve an uncertainty).
11. **Rationale & Next Action**: Formulate execution strategy and trigger conditions.
